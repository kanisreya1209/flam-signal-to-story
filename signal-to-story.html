How it works: The canvas holds every row of xy_data.csv as a particle. "Assemble" eases each point from a burst origin into its true position, revealing a saturation growth curve — fast early gains tapering into a plateau. The orange spline is a Catmull-Rom curve over 30 averaged buckets. "Ask for the story" sends a compact 24-point statistical summary (not 1,500 raw rows) to Claude via a server-side proxy, which writes plain-language narration generated fresh on each request. Built as a portfolio demo for Flam's internship application.
